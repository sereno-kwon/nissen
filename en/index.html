Brand

NISSEN日泉 · Made in Japan

The feel of your brakes
begins with a strand
you never see.

NISSEN (日泉) is a Japanese bicycle cable maker. Originally a maker of springs for weaving looms, the company moved into bicycle brake wire and has focused on bicycle cables ever since, for nearly 70 years. Now that hardly any companies still make cables in Japan, Nissen continues to produce its inner wires and outer housing at home.

A light lever pull, a shift that clicks exactly into place, brakes that stay crisp ride after ride. Everything a rider feels at the fingertips comes from the friction and flexibility of the cable. Nissen is the brand that makes that unseen part as honestly as possible.

Craft

Still wired.

Cables are the least noticeable part of a bicycle, yet the one your hands work most often. Nissen selects stainless steel, twists the strands and finishes the surface to make cables that are flexible and low in friction. Flexible housing reduces friction with the inner wire, making braking and shifting noticeably smoother.

Nissen cables are also introduced to bike shops and builders around the world through SimWorks by Nissen, a line made together with the Japanese bicycle brand SimWorks.

02

Inner Wire

SP31Brake · Shift

31 strands of stainless steel,
smooth all the way to the lever.

SP31 is Nissen's signature inner wire. SUS304 stainless steel is twisted in a 31-strand Warrington construction, closing the gaps between strands and giving a round cross-section. After precision machining for roundness, the surface is plasma plated to cut friction inside the housing even further.

You feel the result at the lever. Brakes pull lighter and more consistently, and shifts land cleanly. Low stretch means your setup stays dialled for longer, another strength of SP31.

Outer Cable

Stainless Outer16 Colors

Colour that shows the coil,
the final line
that completes your bike.

Nissen stainless housing has a translucent colour coating over a stainless coil. You choose a colour, yet the texture of the metal coil still shows through, giving the cockpit a cleaner look. A polyethylene liner inside reduces friction with the inner wire.

5 mm diameter for brakes, 4 mm for shifting. From subtle shades like Clear Black and Clear Brown to Clear Olive, Clear Mustard and Clear Purple, 16 colours let you match your frame, bar tape and grips.

Heritage Outer

French · ItalianReissued classics

Making the cables of the 1950s
again, today.

When restoring a classic bicycle or building one in the spirit of its era, cables are often the last part that is hard to find. Nissen's heritage housing reissues the brake housing used on French and Italian bicycles from the 1950s to the 1970s.

The look of that era, made the way Nissen makes cables today. Two housings suited to randonneurs, vintage road bikes and classic touring bikes.

Choosing the right cable
for your bike.

Brake and shift cables differ in diameter and construction. The basic rule is to choose housing that matches the diameter of your inner wire. At Rubi Workshop we choose the right combination for your bike and install it for you.
