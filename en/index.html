Brand

VelocityGrand Rapids, Michigan

Of all the parts on a bike,
wheels stay with you
the longest.

Velocity started in Brisbane, Australia in 1985 and today makes rims and builds wheels in Grand Rapids, Michigan, USA. Rims are formed, joined and finished in its own factory, and skilled builders complete every wheel by hand, one at a time.

Velocity doesn't change its models every year to chase trends. It keeps making proven rims, sticks to easy-to-source standards, and makes sure a damaged wheel can be repaired and ridden again. From touring bikes and fixed gears to randonneuring and gravel, that is why riders who ride for the long haul choose Velocity.

Built to be ridden, built to be rebuilt.

Wheels that only chase light numbers rarely last a season. Velocity designs rims around riders who ride every day: carrying the weight of a loaded touring bike, the bumps of the city and the shocks of unpaved roads, while holding spoke tension for the long run. That is what Velocity considers a good rim.

The rims are made in an American factory, and the wheels pass through a builder's hands in the same place. Built with standard J-bend spokes and common hub standards, a Velocity wheel can have a spoke replaced or a rim swapped almost anywhere in the world.

02

Road · Randonneur

A23The everyday road rim

Before wide rims were the norm,
the A23 was already wide.

As the name suggests, the A23 is a 23 mm wide road rim. Back when narrow rims were standard, it showed that a wider rim supports the tyre better and rolls more comfortably and efficiently at the same pressure. Today it is the benchmark daily road rim, best matched with 25 to 32 mm tyres.

An asymmetric profile reduces the difference in spoke tension between the two sides of a rear wheel, and the tubeless-ready design lets you ride without tubes. Light without being pared down too far, it can be trusted for long randonnées and light loaded touring.

View at Rubi Workshop →Specification
01

23 mm wide profile

The wide rim gives the tyre a rounder shape and a more stable contact patch, keeping cornering composed even at lower pressures.

02

Tubeless ready

Add tubeless tape and valves and you can ride without tubes. Fewer flats, lower pressures, more comfort.

03

Offset spoke bed

An asymmetric profile with offset spoke holes evens out left and right tension on the rear wheel. The wheel is stiffer and lasts longer.

Urban · Track

Deep VThe icon

An instantly recognisable V-section,
the rim loved by
urban fixed gear.

The Deep V is a rim with a 30 mm deep V-shaped profile. The tall section makes the rim stiff enough to handle skids, jumps and rough city riding, and a single wheel is enough to change the look of a bike. Messengers and fixed-gear riders found it first, and it remains the go-to rim for track bikes and singlespeeds.

Another joy of the Deep V is colour. Beyond black and silver, a range of anodised colours lets you match your frame or deliberately contrast it, for a pair that is yours alone.

04

Touring · Tandem

DyadBuilt for the long way

Fully loaded,
wheels that cross continents.

The Dyad is a double-wall rim built for touring and tandems. It is designed for the bikes that put the heaviest loads on their wheels, like touring bikes with full panniers and tandems carrying two riders. A sturdy profile and generous wall thickness hold spoke tension for the long run and keep the wheel true through long journeys.

Not having to worry about your wheels in the middle of a trip matters more than saving a few grams. The Dyad is a rim for that kind of rider.

05

Gravel · MTB

Blunt SSWide, tough, tubeless

Where the pavement ends,
the real road begins.

The Blunt SS is a disc-only rim for wide tyres. A disc-specific profile with no brake track saves weight, and the wide inner width holds the shape of gravel and MTB tyres steadily. Being tubeless ready, it lets you roll smoothly over rough surfaces at low pressures.

From loaded bikepacking to gravel rides and forest trails. Light, yet hard to bend on rough ground: an off-road rim true to Velocity.

Wheel Build

Custom WheelsBuilt at Rubi Workshop

From rims to hubs and spokes,
a pair of wheels
made for the way you ride.

Rubi Workshop builds custom wheels with Velocity rims. We start by listening: what roads you ride and how much, the weight of rider and luggage, and the tyre width you want. Then we choose the rim, hub, spoke count and lacing pattern together. Every finished wheel is evenly tensioned and stress-relieved before it leaves the shop.

01

Consultation

We listen to your riding style, bike, tyre width, brake type and budget, and recommend the right rim.

02

Choosing parts

Rim model and colour, hub, spoke type and count, even nipple colour: we decide the full spec of your pair.

03

Build

An experienced mechanic laces the wheels by hand, repeating tension measurement and stress relief until everything is even.

04

Delivery and check-up

We fit the wheels and set up the tyres, and after the break-in period we check tension and true again.

Story

1985 ~ 2026Brisbane · Grand Rapids

From a small wheel shop in Australia
to a rim factory in Michigan:
the Velocity story.

Founded in Brisbane, Australia in 1985, Velocity crossed to the United States and settled in Grand Rapids, Michigan, where it began making its own rims. Not flashy, but wheels that everyday riders trust for years. That is the way Velocity has always worked.
